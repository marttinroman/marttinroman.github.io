## User Story

**As a** VP of Engineering or CFO evaluating talent,  
**I want to** see concrete metrics illustrating ROI, defect reduction, and $0 infrastructure hosting cost,  
**So that** I know Marttin aligns engineering architecture directly with business value and cost efficiency.

---

## Scope

### In Scope
- Key metrics callouts:
  - 85% Defect reduction in staging.
  - 10x Faster regression test cycles.
  - $0/mo Infrastructure cost (GitHub Pages, Fastly Edge, Ephemeral GH Actions).
- Responsive layout adapting to mobile screens.

### Out of Scope
- Dynamic telemetry dashboard.

## Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin format)

### Scenario 1: ROI metrics are clearly visible in projects section (Happy Path)
- **Given:** Visitor scrolls to the flagship and project highlights
- **When:** Viewing the metrics callouts
- **Then:** The ROI statistics (85% Defect Reduction, 10x Test Velocity, $0 Infra Cost) are legible with high contrast in both dark and light modes.
